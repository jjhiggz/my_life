## Garden Quiz

1. In Virginia Zone 7a in mid-May, why might basil still sulk after transplanting?  
   Basil likes warm soil and steady warmth; cool nights, wet soil, or transplant shock can slow it down.
2. What is the first thing to check when parsley looks weak in clay-heavy soil?  
   Drainage and moisture balance.
3. Why is cardboard useful in the hill-row expansion zone?  
   It suppresses grass while adding organic matter as it breaks down.
4. For direct-sown sunflower seedlings stuck for a month, what is the practical move?  
   Replace them with stronger tent-grown seedlings.
5. Why do carrots, radishes, beets, and Brussels sprouts help the clay-soil learning goal?  
   Their roots and cultivation help open the soil while showing how the bed handles cool-season crops.
